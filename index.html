01 / THE IDEA

Teach Gemini your
way of working.

A Gem is a version of Gemini you customize with a name and instructions. It can follow your preferred approach for a specific kind of task, and you can return to it whenever you need it.

01

Choose a repeatable task

Look for work where you already follow a pattern: summarize, plan, review, explain, or transform.

02

Write clear instructions

Tell your Gem what it should help with, what steps to follow, and what a useful result looks like.

03

Preview, save, improve

Try a realistic example in the preview. Check the result, refine the instructions, then save your Gem.

About GemsThis workshop uses the Gemini web app. In Gemini, open Gems to create and save a custom Gem. What you can access may depend on your Google account, region, and organization settings.

03 / CREATE A GEM IN THE GEMINI WEB APP

From a new Gem to
your first useful chat.

Open Gemini in your browser, write instructions for the kind of help you want, preview the result, and save your Gem.

01

Open Gemini and find Gems

Go to gemini.google.com and sign in with the Google account you intend to use. Open the sidebar, then choose Gems.

02

Create your Gem

Choose New Gem, give it a name, and add instructions describing its role, the task it should help with, and how you want answers organized.

03

Add context if you need it

You can optionally add reference files under Knowledge if that option is available. Only add information approved for your account and intended use, and review files before uploading.

04

Preview, refine, and save

Try a realistic prompt in the preview. Improve the instructions if needed, then select Save—previewing alone does not save the Gem. Open the saved Gem to start a chat and review its answers.

EXAMPLE GEM INSTRUCTIONS

A clear role.
A repeatable result.

✦ PROMPT COACH
You help me improve prompts for a clear goal.

First ask about the goal and audience.
Point out missing context or constraints.
Offer a revised prompt and explain the changes.
Do not invent details I have not provided.

Use plain language. Be specific about what the Gem should do and the format you find useful. Review and edit the instructions before saving.

04 / DESIGN YOUR OWN GEM

What do you do
the same way every time?

The best first Gem is one you will actually use next week. Capture your goal, your preferred process, what a good answer looks like, and the questions your Gem should ask.

TRY THIS IN THE GEMINI WEB APP

Help me design instructions for a Gem that supports this repeatable task: [describe the task].

Ask me five questions about the goal, audience, inputs, steps, preferred answer format, and edge cases. Then draft a Gem name and clear instructions I can review and paste into the Gemini web app. Keep them practical and easy to test. Do not invent details.
